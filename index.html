Who We Are

Afriglobal Resources Limited is a privately held Tanzanian mineral resource company extracting and commercializing precious metals, base metals, and industrial minerals. We operate two active gold production sites in Chunya (Mbeya) and Kilindi (Tanga), with garnet extraction from Korogwe (Tanga).

Our multi-commodity portfolio—gold, copper, and industrial garnet—provides natural hedging. As a TIC registered entity with full Mining Commission licensing, we operate within a robust regulatory framework.
